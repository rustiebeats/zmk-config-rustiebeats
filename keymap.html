How the keys behave

Home-row mods
Balanced flavour, 280 ms hold, 175 ms quick-tap. Needs 125 ms of idle first, only fires against the opposite hand or a thumb, and decides on release — so rolls never turn into modifiers.
Layer taps
Balanced flavour, 200 ms hold, 175 ms quick-tap.
Combos
20 ms window for same-row chords, 30 ms for the vertical ones, after 100 ms of idle.
Sticky shift
Holds for 2 s or until the next key, with quick-release so it never doubles up.
Caps word
Survives ⌫ ⏎ ⌦ _ −. ignore-numbers is deleted, so a digit ends it.
Key repeat
Repeats consumer keys as well as normal ones.
Morphing keys
Three keys change under shift: , becomes ;, . becomes :, and ? becomes !
Power & radio
Sleeps after 30 min idle. Up to 4 Bluetooth connections and 4 pairings. USB logging off.
